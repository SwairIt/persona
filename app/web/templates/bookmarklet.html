Browser bookmarklet

One-click capture of the page you're on — URL, title, and your selected text — into a Persona note.

1. Drag this to your bookmarks bar

Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows / Linux) or Cmd+Shift+B (macOS) first if your bookmarks bar is hidden.

+ Save to Persona Drag → don't click.

2. How to use it

  1. Visit any page you want to remember.
  2. Optionally select a passage of text you want to quote.
  3. Click Save to Persona in your bookmarks bar.
  4. A small confirmation banner appears in the corner for ~2 seconds, then disappears.
  5. Open /inbox to see the new note.

What the note looks like
