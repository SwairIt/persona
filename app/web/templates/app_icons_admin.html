App icons

Override the auto-generated tile for any app — handy for Electron wrappers, in-house tools and remote-desktop windows where the Win32 extractor can't see a real exe icon. Uploaded PNGs are stored inside the SQLite file so they travel with your backups.

Limits: PNG only, max 256 KB, max 256x256. Anything smaller is upscaled to the 64x64 timeline tile on the fly. Hit Reset to drop the row and let the auto-generator pick the icon on the next request.

Use after changing the initials algorithm. Custom uploads are kept.
