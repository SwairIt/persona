Multi-key sequences are two whitespace-separated tokens (e.g. g t — press g, then t within 1.5 seconds). A single token like ? binds an instant key.

Stored in kv_settings under kbd_shortcuts_json. The front-end fetches /api/kbd-shortcuts.json once on load and falls back to the built-in defaults if the request fails.

Browser-reserved combinations (Ctrl+F, Cmd+P, Esc, etc.) are never overridden — only the single-letter and g-prefixed sequence shortcuts listed above.
