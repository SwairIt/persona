Combine both buckets — every skipped and failed row goes back to pending.

Affects up to {{ counts.skipped + counts.failed }} row(s).

Tip: you can also reset a single screenshot via POST /api/screenshots/<id>/reset-ocr, or from the terminal with persona reset-ocr --scope all.
