⚠ OCR find & replace

Bulk Python re.sub across screenshots.ocr_text. Use this to repair systematic Tesseract mis-reads, redact a leaked secret that landed in OCR text, or normalise a name that came out three different ways. There is no undo — preview first.

Heads up. Every Apply rewrites the canonical ocr_text column for matched rows. Search, embeddings, exports and digests will reflect the new text immediately. The action is audit-logged under ocr.find_replace — the full pattern, replacement, scanned count and changed count are captured for forensics.

Preview is always dry-run — no rows are touched until you click Apply on the result below.
