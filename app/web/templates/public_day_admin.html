How it works

  • Slug grammar is ^[a-z0-9-]{1,60}$ — lowercase letters, digits, hyphens.
  • Re-publishing the same day overwrites the slug, title, and blurb.
  • The public page filters screenshots tagged private or confidential and masks OCR text via your redaction rules before rendering.
  • Annotations are never shown publicly. Per-screenshot notes are shown but redacted.
  • Unpublishing deletes the row immediately — anyone with the link gets a 404 on the next request.
