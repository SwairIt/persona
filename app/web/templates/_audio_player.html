Context contract ---------------- * ``seg`` — dict with ``id`` (int) and ``duration_seconds`` (float). The id drives the stream URL. * ``peaks`` — list[float] in ``[0.0, 1.0]``; may be empty when soundfile/numpy isn't installed or the file is missing on disk.
