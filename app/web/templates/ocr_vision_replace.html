OCR vision replace

Promote the v0.55 multimodal-vision transcription (ocr_text_vision) into the canonical Tesseract column (ocr_text) for every shot that has both. Use this when you've spot-checked the vision pass on the OCR via vision page and decided the vision result is better than the Tesseract one across the board.

This is destructive — Tesseract's existing text is overwritten, not appended. The sidecar ocr_text_vision column is left in place so a future re-promotion is still possible, but the previous Tesseract text is gone after the swap.
