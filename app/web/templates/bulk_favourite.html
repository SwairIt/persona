★ Bulk favourite

Apply the ★ favourite mark to every screenshot matching a full-text search query — favourites surface on /favourites as a quick-recall grid. Distinct from bulk pin: pinning protects shots from retention demotion, while favouriting is purely a discovery shortcut. A shot may be one, both, or neither.

Heads up. Favouriting is non-destructive but a runaway query (query=".") can star thousands of shots in one click and turn the favourites grid into noise. Always preview first to see the match count. The confirm button only appears after a preview, and the confirmation token is invalidated as soon as the query or match count changes.

Preview is always dry-run — nothing is starred yet.

Strips the ★ from every matching shot. No preview step — re-favouriting is one form away.
