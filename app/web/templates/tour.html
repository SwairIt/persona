Welcome to Persona

{# Annotated mock of /timeline. SVG so we don't need a real screenshot asset — arrows point at scrubber + thumbnails + nav, the three things a first-time user has to find. #}

The Timeline is where every screenshot lives. Drag the scrubber to jump in time, click any thumbnail to open the full frame, or use the top nav to switch into Ask, Memory or Settings. Everything stays on this machine.

{# Step 2 — Ask. #}
2

Ask anything

Natural-language questions over your own history.

/ask
> What was that JIRA ticket I had open last Tuesday afternoon?
← PERSONA-1423, opened 14:18, closed 17:02. 14 related frames.

Ask runs a natural-language query against your OCR'd captures. It works best when you bring your own LLM key (Anthropic, OpenAI, Groq, or Gemini) — you'll set that up on step 5. Without a key, Ask falls back to keyword search.

{# Step 3 — Memory. #}

Memory cards & daily pins

Memory generates an hourly card for each block of activity and lets you pin the highlights of the day. Both are stored as plain rows you can edit, export, or delete at any time.

{# Step 5 — LLM setup. #}
5

Connect your LLM key

Bring your own model — pay no extra middleman.

Persona supports four providers out of the box:
  • Anthropic Claude
  • OpenAI GPT
  • Groq
  • Google Gemini

Head back to the setup wizard to paste your key. If you tick the master-password box, Persona stores it in the encrypted vault; otherwise it lives in the local kv table.
